THE BASICS

Before you toss it, check this.

Singapore uses commingled recycling: accepted paper, plastic, metal and glass items go together in the blue bin. Keep food and liquid out. Batteries and electronics need a dedicated e-waste collection point. Composting is only an option if you have a suitable composting setup.

🔵

Blue recycling bin

For accepted clean, dry paper, plastic, metal and glass. Empty containers; rinse only if there is residue. Flatten clean cardboard.

🌱

Compost at home

Fruit and vegetable scraps can go into a suitable home compost system. Without one, use general waste; don’t put food in the blue bin.

🔋

Special drop-off

Small household batteries and e-waste need dedicated collection points. Never put batteries or electronics in the blue recycling bin.

💰

Return Right · 10¢

Eligible plastic and metal drink containers with the BCRS deposit mark can be returned for 10¢. Keep them empty, uncrushed, and barcode-visible.

🧺

Clothing & textiles

Cloop textile bins accept clean, dry, reusable items from their accepted list. Check the list and location before going.

Based on NEA public guidance. This prototype is educational, not an official disposal service. Source details are listed in the project README.
